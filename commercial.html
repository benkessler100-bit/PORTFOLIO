WHO I AM — a short story

I'm Chen — a photographer and videographer based in Tel Aviv, with a passport that doesn't sit still. My week is split between the kitchens, dance floors, studios, and racetracks of Israel — and the months a year I spend somewhere I don't speak the language.

Plates, faces, motion, light. I shoot what'll outlive the moment. Selected work: brand films for Chicode and Viacci · restaurant content for Bar Giora & Saba Edward · the Petzael × Sparco track-day aftermovie · a music video for Maya Singer · portraits for musicians and founders across Israel.
