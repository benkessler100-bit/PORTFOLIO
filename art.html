WHO I AM — a short story

I'm Chen — a filmmaker and photographer living in Tel Aviv, where I study film at Tel Aviv University. I write, shoot, and edit short films — and between productions I carry a stills camera everywhere the scripts haven't reached yet.

This side of the archive is the personal one — short films written and cut between semesters, and the stills that feed them: mountain mornings in the Annapurna, 3am fog in Kyoto, the Negev crater at first light, Mongolian steppe under a sky too big for one frame. Unhurried, no client brief.

PORTRAIT — Studio · EPK · Press

Stripped-down studio portraits — clean light, real expressions. EPKs, press kits, album covers — frames where the face has to do everything. Recent subjects: musicians, founders, athletes — composed cleanly for whatever crop a magazine throws at it.

TRAVEL — Asia & the Desert

Eight months on the road across Asia in 2024 — Japan, India, Mongolia, Vietnam, Nepal, Laos, Thailand, Taiwan. Plus the deserts of Mizpe Ramon back home, and a side-quest into Japanese kei cars that turned into its own mini-series.

BEHIND THE SCENES

Every photo on this site has a story behind it — the 4am hike for one minute of golden hour, the chef who wouldn't stop refilling the plate, the bartender who set the bar on fire on cue, the corner of a Mongolian field that worked because we waited an hour for the cloud to move. The behind-the-scenes is the part I keep for the long cold nights of editing.
